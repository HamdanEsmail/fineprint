6 Nov 2026, 14:14 UTCClaude release notes

The sign‑out capability for Claude Enterprise members was changed: the new description no longer mentions ending sign‑in sessions, trusted devices, or OAuth tokens, and the specific beta header admin-user-sign-out-2026-10-08 was removed.

Before · recorded 6 Nov 2026, 02:14 UTC

You can now sign a member of your Claude Enterprise (claude.ai) organization out of Claude everywhere with the Admin API (see the Admin API reference). POST /v1/organizations/users/{user_id}/sign_out is in beta, and every request must include the admin-user-sign-out-2026-10-08 beta header. It ends the member’s sign-in sessions and trusted devices on every browser and device, and their OAuth tokens for your organization. The member stays in the organization and can sign in again right away. See Sign a member out everywhere for the key scope that the request needs and for what it doesn’t end.

After · recorded 6 Nov 2026, 14:14 UTC

You can now sign a member of your Claude Enterprise (claude.ai) organization out of Claude everywhere with the Admin API. This capability is in beta. See the Admin API reference. The member stays in the organization and can sign in again. See Sign a member out everywhere for the beta header and what the request doesn’t end.
